title: UK AI Safety Institute
subtitle: A UK government research body that evaluates advanced AI systems for capability and safety risks.

The UK AI Safety Institute (AISI, renamed AI Security Institute in early 2025) operates within the UK's Department for Science, Innovation and Technology and was the first state-backed organisation dedicated to evaluating advanced AI systems. It runs pre-deployment evaluations of frontier models — through agreements with developers including OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Meta — on capability, bio/chemical/cyber misuse risk, and societal-impact dimensions.

AISI publishes evaluation methodology and selected results, contributes to international evaluation standards, and serves as a working model for state-led AI assessment that other governments (US AISI, EU AI Office) have followed.
