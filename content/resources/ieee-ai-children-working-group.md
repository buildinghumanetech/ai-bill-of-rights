---
title: IEEE AI & Children working group
subtitle: An IEEE Standards Association working group developing engineering standards for AI used by or for children.
sourceUrl: https://standards.ieee.org/
---

The IEEE AI & Children working group — part of the IEEE Standards Association's Global Initiative on Ethics of Autonomous and Intelligent Systems — develops engineering standards aimed at protecting minors in AI-mediated experiences. Output includes draft standards on age-appropriate explainability, ethical content generation, and prohibitions on dependency-engineered design.

These standards are referenced in regulatory frameworks across the EU, UK, and US, and are intended to give product teams concrete, testable requirements rather than abstract principles.
